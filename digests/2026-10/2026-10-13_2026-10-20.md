From CAMO's perspective on the statistical and algorithmic foundations of trustworthy AI, this is a concrete instance of a broader problem in algorithmic decision-making — optimizing a system on aggregate outcomes can systematically disadvantage minority subgroups in ways that aggregate metrics conceal. **Key takeaway:** Fine-tuning LLMs for cultural alignment raises average accuracy but widens performance gaps between demographic subgroups, meaning organizations that rely on a single accuracy number to validate culturally adapted AI may be deploying systems that are quietly less fair than their predecessors.
